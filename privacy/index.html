Privacy Policy

Instarep.ly is designed as a local iOS keyboard workflow: copy text, generate a reply on device with Apple Intelligence, review it, and send only if you choose.

What Instarep.ly uses

  • Copied text: the comment or message you copy is used to generate the reply you request.
  • Profiles and tone settings: the app may store your chosen profiles, tones, and instructions on your device so replies match your workflow.
  • Purchase status: Apple handles in-app purchases and may provide the app with entitlement status so the free trial and unlimited unlock work.

Full Access for the keyboard

iOS requires third-party keyboards to request Full Access for clipboard-based workflows. That permission is broad and can allow a keyboard extension to process text while it is active. Instarep.ly uses it to read copied text for the reply you request; only enable it if you are comfortable with Apple’s third-party keyboard model.

On-device generation

The App Store listing states that Instarep.ly generates replies locally with Apple Intelligence and does not use a backend AI service for remote reply generation. Copied text is used for the reply you request, not to run a hosted chatbot account.

Accounts, payments, and support

Instarep.ly does not require an account. App Store downloads, purchases, refunds, billing details, and payment methods are handled by Apple. If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond to that request.

This website

This marketing site is static and does not include a contact form. Hosting providers may process standard technical logs such as IP address, user agent, request URL, and timestamp for security, reliability, and abuse prevention.

Children

Instarep.ly is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us and we will delete it where required.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. For privacy questions, email support@isolated.tech.
