The Full Access switch, explained plainly.

Apple shows a broad warning for every third-party keyboard. Here is what Instarep.ly needs it for.

Why it is needed

Clipboard access

Instarep.ly generates from text you intentionally copy. Full Access lets the keyboard read that copied comment while the keyboard is active.

What can be accessed

Text while the keyboard is active

Full Access is a broad iOS keyboard permission. Instarep.ly’s workflow uses your copied text and selected profile/tone; it does not need your social login or account credentials.

Where generation happens

On your device

The App Store listing states that replies are generated locally with Apple Intelligence and that there is no backend AI service for remote reply generation.

Your responsibility

Review the output

AI replies can be wrong or off-tone. Treat every draft as a starting point and only post messages you are comfortable sending.

Honest limitation: iOS grants third-party keyboards a sensitive permission here, so only enable Full Access if you are comfortable with that model. Avoid using any third-party keyboard for sensitive entry. Instarep.ly is designed around copied text, local generation, and no account-based backend.

Questions buyers ask before installing.

Straight answers for privacy, compatibility, pricing, and daily use.

Why does the keyboard need Full Access?

Instarep.ly needs to read the text you copied so it can generate a reply from that context. Apple uses a broad warning for all third-party keyboards with Full Access, so the permission looks scarier than the actual Instarep.ly workflow.

Does my comment text leave my device?

The App Store listing says reply generation happens on device with Apple Intelligence, with no backend AI service or remote reply generation. The copied text is used for the reply you request.

Which apps does it support?

Instarep.ly works in apps that allow standard iOS keyboard input, including social comments, DMs, messaging, support tools, and other text fields. App-specific restrictions can still apply.

Can I edit the reply before it posts?

Yes. Treat every generated reply as a draft. You can send it as-is, rewrite it, add details, or skip it entirely.

What device or iOS version do I need?

The App Store currently lists iOS 26.0 or later. Because generation uses Apple Intelligence, use a compatible device and check the App Store compatibility panel before installing.

How does pricing work?

You get 20 successful generated replies free. After that, the current release notes describe a one-time in-app upgrade for unlimited replies. The App Store shows the exact price for your region before purchase.
