Quick Setup

Get started in under 60 seconds.

1

Download the app

Get instarep.ly from the App Store. It's free to download.

2

Enable the keyboard

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → instarep.ly

Settings → General → Keyboard → Add New
3

Allow full access

This lets the keyboard read your clipboard. Your data stays on-device.

4

Start replying!

Switch keyboards with the globe icon. Copy a comment and watch the magic happen.
